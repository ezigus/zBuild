- answered: lens-correctness-design finding 1: wrong — The design already addresses this. Gap 11 documents the problem: after `skip_until_stage` is cleared when the from-stage target is found (in both dispatch-unit arms of `main()` in runner.sh), `resume_mode` is immediately also set to `false`. This prevents the resume skip guard (`$resume_mode && [[ -z "$skip_until_stage" ]]`) from activating for all subsequent leaf stages after `<s>`. DSFSTG-3[code] pins the observable behavior: stages after `<s>` that are marked complete are still dispatched, and no stage.skip event is emitted for any stage after `<s>`. Finding 31 and the Decision paragraph both document this explicitly. The fix is `[[ -n "$skip_until_stage" ]] && { skip_until_stage=""; resume_mode=false; }` in both arms.
